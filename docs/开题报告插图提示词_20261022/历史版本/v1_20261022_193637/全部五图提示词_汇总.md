An attacker can modify only one source document; victim-side chunking produces two distinct, semantically complementary chunks that may jointly enter a retrieved context. All labels must be readable when the figure is inserted at about 17 cm width; rebalance geometry rather than shrinking text. Keep all text horizontal.
