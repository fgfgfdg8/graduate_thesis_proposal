# CIKM 2026 RAG/Security Candidate Scan

## Scope and evidence level

- 核查日期：2026-11-08。入口为 [CIKM 2026 官方程序](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/program/)，定向检索其 RAG、Adversarial Attacks & Backdoors、LLM Agents & Memory、GraphRAG、poisoning、citation、chunk continuity 与 evidence-pruning 摘要；这是高相关筛查，不宣称逐篇阅读全文或穷尽整个10,245行程序页。
- CIKM 按用户提供的《第七版中国计算机学会推荐国际学术会议和期刊目录》列为CCF-B会议；Short等非Full/Regular论文不计入该目录所指的会议论文范围。下列条目除另注外均仅L1（官方程序元数据/摘要），不把摘要改写成正文结论。
- ACM DOI 页面/全文端点经当前检索通道不可读取。CaseFlow 有作者程序所链接的公开实现，但仓库 README/代码不等于论文全文；CARE 仓库页面仍写“Code coming soon”。本轮未下载任何 CIKM PDF。

## Highest-priority candidates

| Candidate | Official abstract evidence | Thesis overlap and boundary | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cite Unseen: Measuring Citation-Channel Vulnerabilities in Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Minseok Hur, Jiho Shin, Damin Kim, Moohong Min; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3839886` | Across four BEIR corpora, three retrievers and four generators, it changes only the source URL of one injected document; official abstract reports up to 71% citation-stage ASR at under 1% corpus pollution. | Most direct CIKM attack-side RAG neighbor: it treats corpus insertion and retrieved-document source identity as an attack surface, but targets unsafe citation routing while answer text remains largely unchanged. It does not establish false-answer poisoning, a single true long source split by an unknown victim chunker, complementary same-source chunks, or joint necessity. Its C-ASR also shows why answer ASR alone is not a complete safety endpoint. | L1; full text not obtained. The CIKM program author order differs from one university-hosted page; retain this metadata conflict until ACM/Crossref record reconciliation. Highest priority for publisher-full-text retrieval and comparison. |
| **SCAR: Semantic Continuity-Aware Retrieval for Efficient Context Expansion in RAG** — Nathanaël Langlois; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3839919`; arXiv:2606.16661v2 | Full 5-page arXiv v2 read (L3). On 160 boundary-fragmented queries, adaptive adjacent-chunk expansion returns 92.8% gold-chunk recall with 7.84 chunks versus static ±1 windowing's 96.7% with 10.16 chunks, a 22.9% chunk reduction and 3.9-point recall cost. | Directly constrains broad novelty around chunk-boundary fragmentation, adjacent evidence recovery, and clean multi-hop context preservation. It is not an attack or detector: no malicious source, poisoning labels, provenance judgment, non-adjacent evidence, or same-source complementary-payload necessity. | L3 for arXiv v2 only; not yet compared line-by-line to ACM VoR. See [SCAR full-text note](20261108_SCAR_2606.16661.md). |
| **FortMem: Secure Stateful Data Management for Long-Term LLM Agent Memory** — Zening Li, Hongchao Qin, Guang Zeng, Rong-Hua Li; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840872` | Official abstract proposes zero-trainable-parameter PASTP trust propagation over a provenance-anchored signed ANN–NLI corroboration graph, plus PASTP+ demotion for spoofed provenance. It reports 96.3% poisoned-snapshot detection with zero false alarms. | Strong adjacent defense neighbor for poisoning and provenance-weighted trust. The object is persistent Agent memory records across sessions, not an external RAG knowledge base with unknown source-document chunking; the abstract does not establish candidate-level or final query-level false-removal denominators. | L1; claims/denominators require full-paper verification. |
| **Do RAG Systems Cite Themselves? Measuring and Preventing Source Displacement in Self-Updating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (LineageGuard)** — Yajuan Wang; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3841081` | Official program abstract reports controlled 100-round streams on HotpotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA: unrestricted reuse places unverified generated memories in 27.6%–28.4% of late-stage Top-5 slots and produces citation-lineage collapse in 10.7%–11.7% of answers. With correct verifier/lineage metadata, the policy enforces zero exposure/collapse; primary-source recall rises 5.0 points on HotpotQA while answer F1 falls on 2Wiki. An audit of 150 admitted memories reports 91.3% joint support-lineage precision. | Strong B reference for provenance lineage, self-reinforcing generated memory, and distinguishing primary evidence from model-produced text. Not a poison detector or chunk-level attack; source displacement is an information-lineage failure rather than malicious false-fact injection. | L1; full text and all denominators/configuration details not checked. A separate university-hosted page previously surfaced 22.8%–41.9%; that is not the official program's stated 100-round result and remains an unresolved version/setup discrepancy, not a substitute value. |
| **PCD-RAG: A Diagnostic Framework for Auditing Parametric Contamination in RAG Distillation** — Yingying Wu, Xiao Chen, Changyi Ma, Xuan Song; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3839997` | Official abstract proposes a negative-control benchmark using 5,000 synthetic facts with zero pretraining exposure to distinguish context-conditioned reading from parametric memorization. It reports retrieval-free answer recovery at or below 0.8% after answer-only distillation in that setting, plus a noisy multi-document distillation study. | Directly relevant to whether retrieved transient facts can cross the retrieval/generation boundary and contaminate model parameters. It studies distillation/training-time contamination, not an attacker poisoning a fixed deployed RAG corpus, retrieval-block composition, or online detector calibration. | L1; full paper/data not obtained. Compare threat model and contamination measurement before treating it as overlap or as evidence against corpus-attack feasibility. |
| **MemDTG: Dynamic Temporal Memory Graphs for Evidence-Admissible Agent Memory** — Yaxin Deng, Hongchao Qin, Tianyi Gao, Guang Zeng, Rong-Hua Li; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3841052` | Official abstract separates immutable episodes, temporal entity-state graph and memory-evolution graph, then applies query-time temporal/role constraints before candidate ranking and outputs source/role certificates. It reports fewer stale-answer and future-leak errors. | Adjacent to B's evidence admissibility and source traceability, but it governs temporal agent memory validity, not malicious RAG poisoning, fixed chunk features, or false-positive calibration. | L1; full text not obtained. |
| **Causality-Guided Spurious Evidence Pruning with GFlowNet for Faithful Graph Retrieval (CaseFlow)** — Jinho Choi et al.; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3841152` | Official abstract says it replaces a path-level weakly supervised GraphRAG candidate generator with a flow-guided pruner, uses a relation-conditioned disentanglement encoder as a causal-relevance proxy and adaptive relation-coherent chunks, and evaluates WebQSP/CWQ/MetaQA-3hop. Code: [author repository](https://github.com/ChoiPi2/CaseFlow). | Strong B method/evaluation neighbor: it explicitly removes answer-reaching but non-causal triples while preserving answer-supporting structure, challenging broad claims of novelty around pruning spurious evidence without losing multi-hop support. It is not a detector for adversarially poisoned corpus chunks or source authenticity. | L1; repo is not a substitute for paper. |
| **Retriever as an Attack Surface: Exploring Fairness Vulnerabilities in Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Yuzhe Liu et al.; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3841088` | Official abstract formalizes AFA-RAG/AFA-RL: adversary modifies retriever parameters only, keeps corpus and generator fixed, and shifts evidence exposure toward a target demographic under relevance/stealth constraints. | Attack-surface boundary reference for retriever supply-chain and ranking manipulation. It is neither corpus poisoning nor same-source cross-chunk composition; the fairness objective differs from the proposal's target false-fact answer. | L1; full text not obtained. |
| **Assessing Attack Surfaces in Generative Search Engines through Publisher Attributes: A Case Study in Political Domains** — Riku Mochizuki et al.; CIKM 2026 Full; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840597`; arXiv:2608.15814 | Official abstract studies citation selection and personalization and proposes a content-injection-barrier measure for GSE publisher authority. | Adjacent web-search poisoning-surface measurement, not a fixed external knowledge-base attack or a cross-chunk defense. Useful to distinguish publisher/source authority from the proposal's chunk-level signals. | L1; full text not read in this scan. |

## Method and evaluation references

| Candidate | Relevance | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **State-Conditioned Evidence Coalition Selection for Multi-Hop Retrieval-Augmented Question Answering (MECS)** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840801` | Strong clean multi-hop control: marginal evidence value changes with the already-selected coalition; supports complementary bridge evidence rather than isolated relevance scoring. It does not detect malicious content. | CIKM program abstract only, L1. |
| **QFR-RAG: A Multi-Level Evaluation Resource for Grounded RAG over Technical-Medical Documentation** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840207` | Benchmark-design reference: 50 extraction, 50 multi-evidence, and 300 paired adversarial items, with retrieval sufficiency, completeness, citation grounding, abstention and safety-preserving correction/refusal separated. Not an attack or defense method. | CIKM program abstract only, L1. |
| **CARE: When Not to Retrieve—Conflict-Aware Empathetic Dialogue via Intent-Gated Retrieval and Latent Critique** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840875` | Learns when to withhold retrieved material under injected noise and emotional conflict. Rejection is a useful policy baseline, but emotional misalignment is not factual source poisoning or evidence authenticity. | CIKM abstract L1; author repo says implementation is not yet available. |
| **GraphLoom: Reliability-Calibrated Graph Evidence Routing for Multimodal KG-RAG** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840650`; **GraFine: Retrieval-Time Refinement for Efficient Graph RAG over Corpus Graphs** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840565` | Graph evidence routing/pruning under noisy or distractor pools; useful comparators for graph-structured retrieval and evidence budget, not security claims. | Official abstracts L1. |
| **Coverage Under Contradiction: A Benchmark for Negation-Aware Retrieval** — Pasha Abrishamchian, Eugene Yang, Andrew Yates, Nazli Goharian, Ophir Frieder; CIKM 2026 Resource; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840200` | CoverageBench-Negate edits support documents to controlled negated counterparts across six datasets and about 63,000 original/edited evidence pairs; official abstract reports BM25 separates original vs. edited evidence much better than dense retrievers, which remain near chance. | Directly relevant evaluation resource for retrieval under corpus-vs-parametric-knowledge contradiction and preserving evidence coverage. It is not a poisoning attack: the benchmark's negation/edit construction and relevance labels must be checked before mapping to malicious false-fact insertion or query-level deletion costs. | L1; official program abstract only; resource/repo and full paper not yet inspected. |
| **RankGap-RAG: Rank-Discrepancy Contrastive Feedback for Multi-Paraphrase Retrieval** — Jiae Lee, Seunghyun Yoon, Hyuk Lim; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3839890` | Official abstract identifies iterative retrieval error amplification when misleading retrieved passages are reused as feedback; the method uses bi-/cross-encoder rank discrepancy to select hard-negative feedback and reports gains on five QA datasets. | Relevant to the proposal's attacker-controlled or misleading retrieved evidence feedback loop and retrieval-stage controls. It is a query-refinement method, not a corpus-poisoning detector or provenance defense; negative feedback could also suppress legitimate evidence and needs clean-query calibration. | L1; official program abstract only; full paper not obtained. |
| **Beyond One-Shot Expansion: Contrastive Evidence Exploration for Multi-Hop Retrieval** — Jungmin Yun, Youngbin Kim; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840072` | Official abstract describes evidence-conditioned iterative exploration, passage-specific contrastive facets, and coverage-aware ranking on MuSiQue, HotpotQA, and 2WikiMultiHopQA. | Clean multi-hop comparator for complementary passage selection and context coverage, useful to constrain broad claims about multi-evidence retrieval. Not a security/poisoning method; assess whether its contrastive facets rely on offline corpus structure unavailable in the proposal setting. | L1; official program abstract only; full paper not obtained. |
| **CiteAlign-Table: Verifying Citation Faithfulness when LLMs Attribute to Structured Data** — Ashish Gupta, Manjunatha Naik Mc; CIKM 2026 Short; DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840003` | Official abstract targets wrong-row, unsupported aggregation, and schema/claim mismatch in table-grounded attributed QA; reports 0.84 F1 versus 0.61 for text-only baselines on its hybrid benchmark. | Low-priority evaluation/method reference for separating retrieved-source correctness from fine-grained claim-to-evidence alignment. Structured table attribution is outside the proposal's unstructured malicious chunk setting. | L1; official program abstract only; full paper and benchmark not obtained. |
| **The Compaction Cliff in Long-Running AI Agent Memory** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840567`; **H-Mem: Hawkes-driven Adaptive Forgetting for Long-Horizon Agent Memory** — DOI `10.1145/3799682.3840962` | Safety-rule retention and multi-event memory evidence preservation are adjacent agent-memory work. Neither abstracts a poisoned RAG corpus or source-document chunking. | Official abstracts L1. |
The official 3C Retrieval-Augmented Generation session also includes BRAD (code summarization), GeoRisk-RAG (geographic applicability/abstention), Predicting the Benefit of Retrieval (query-level retrieval/no-retrieval routing), and LLM-Specific Utility for RAG. These are lower-priority non-security controls; their abstracts do not indicate corpus-poisoning defenses. They remain screened-out leads rather than full-text exclusions.
