## Mechanism and evidence

DrAttack decomposes a malicious prompt into sub-prompts, implicitly reconstructs the intent through in-context examples that are semantically similar but benign, then searches for synonyms that retain intent while reducing harmful-word salience. The formal Findings PDF reports 80% success on GPT-4 and a 65-percentage-point improvement over prior methods.

## Version and relevance boundaries

The formal PDF's 80% / 65-point figures differ from the 78% / 33.1-point figures on the current arXiv/project presentation. Keep results version-specific and cite the formal publication figures only when describing the Findings version.

This is a query/prompt jailbreak, not persistent RAG corpus poisoning: it does not manipulate a retriever, victim chunker, or retrieved knowledge chunks. It is an indirect conceptual neighbor for decomposition/reconstruction, not evidence that the proposed single-source, unknown-chunker, jointly necessary chunk attack has already been demonstrated.
